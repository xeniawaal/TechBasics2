# Learning Log – week 2

## What I Expected

I expected to learn more about the breadboard and how to connect different lights. Since I have very little experience with electronics, I thought working with several LEDs might be a bit confusing, but I was curious to try it out.

## How It Turned Out

I finally received my own DIY kit, but after unpacking it, I realised that it was too small and some components were missing. So, I will keep the one that I borrowed to have both options. 

During the session, we learned about resistors, their colour codes and why they are needed to protect LEDs. We also discussed direct and alternating current. We connected red, yellow and green LEDs and programmed them to light up together and in sequence. Afterwards, we used an RGB LED and a custom function to mix different colours.

## What I Learned

I learned how to control LEDs using `digitalWrite()` and how to adjust brightness and mix colours using `analogWrite()`. Defining the pins as variables also made the code easier to understand. I also learned why it is important to avoid directly connecting the positive and negative terminals.

## My Impression

I am starting to feel more comfortable with breadboards and circuits. Some parts are still new and confusing to me, but seeing the lights react to the code makes learning more enjoyable. I especially liked experimenting with the RGB LED because I could immediately see how different values changed the colour.
